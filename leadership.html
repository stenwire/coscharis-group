Dr. Cosmas Maduka is the founder, President, and CEO of Coscharis Group. An industrialist and visionary leader, he has guided the company from its humble beginnings to a renowned transnational conglomerate. His entrepreneurial spirit and unwavering commitment to the "value for money" philosophy remain the cornerstones of the Group's success and enduring relevance.

He is a respected voice in African business, celebrated for his integrity, and serves as a mentor to a new generation of entrepreneurs.

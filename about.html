About Coscharis Group

We are a proudly Nigerian and transnational institution with a 45-year legacy of delivering excellence, integrity, and true value to our customers.

Our Story

Founded in 1977 by the visionary entrepreneur Dr. Cosmas Maduka, Coscharis Group began as a modest enterprise focused on automotive spare parts. Fueled by a relentless commitment to quality and customer satisfaction, the Group has grown exponentially into one of Nigeria's most diversified and respected conglomerates.

Today, our interests span multiple key sectors of the economy, including Automotive, Technology, Mobility, Agriculture, and Healthcare. While our businesses are diverse, they are unified by a central philosophy: to build timeless institutions that deliver exceptional value and contribute meaningfully to the progress of our nation and continent.

Our Vision

To build an institution that will be timeless in its relevance and value.

Our Mission

To deliver quality life and automotive solutions to our stakeholders through our dedicated and highly motivated staff.

Our Brand Promise

"Value for Money"

This is not just a slogan; it is the core of our operational philosophy. It is our unwavering commitment to every customer, partner, and stakeholder. It means delivering superior quality, reliability, and service at a fair price, ensuring that every interaction with Coscharis Group is a rewarding one.

Delivering value for money, since 1977.
